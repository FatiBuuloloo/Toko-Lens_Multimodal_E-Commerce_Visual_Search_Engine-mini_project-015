📸 Photo Guide

  • Place the item in a good position — on a flat, clean surface.
  • Ensure sufficient lighting; natural light is highly recommended.
  • Avoid including other objects around the item you want to search.
  • Keep your hands and shadows out of the photo frame.
  • Use a plain background to make the item stand out clearly.
  • Take the photo straight from the front, avoid tilted or side angles.
  • Ensure the item fills most of the frame without being cut off.
